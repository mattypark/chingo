# ChinGo mascot — 12 prompts

Paste each block into ChatGPT (GPT Image) one at a time. One image per prompt.

**The mascot is one character: a round bear cub.** All twelve are pictures of the *same* cub —
what changes is silhouette weight, which colour carries the body versus the accent, ear and
muzzle proportion, expression, and posture. Not twelve different animals.

**Six are full-body and uncropped** (F1–F6): the whole cub, centred, nothing cut off at any
edge. **Six emerge from a bottom corner** (C1–C6): three from the lower-left, three from the
lower-right.

Every prompt asks for exactly three colours in the image — two on the character, one solid
background — because a closed palette is what makes a mark read as designed rather than
generated. The palette moves across the batch on purpose so you can see which pairing carries
the brand.

## Saving the results

Save each into this folder with the matching name, so the prompt and its output stay paired:

```
assets/logo/F1.png   assets/logo/F2.png   …   assets/logo/F6.png
assets/logo/C1.png   assets/logo/C2.png   …   assets/logo/C6.png
```

Then tell me which you want and I'll cut it into the app icon, the splash mascot, and the catch
button glyph.

## A note on judging them

Squint at each one, or shrink it to the size of a tab bar icon. If the silhouette stops being
readable, it fails — no amount of charm at full size survives being 32 pixels tall on someone's
home screen.

```
Create one complete full-bleed 1:1 square image.
Background: fill the entire square with a solid, gently muted sky blue. Keep that blue visible in every open area and in all four corners.
Subject: one extremely simplified, cute, endearing round bear cub, reduced to a single soft rounded continuous silhouette — a large head, two small round ears, a compact body.

Colour: exactly three colours in the whole image — a deep berry purple carrying the body, a warm cream for the belly, muzzle and inner ears, plus the background. Reuse those same two character colours for the facial marks. Keep character, facial marks and background clearly separated.
Composition: the entire cub upright, complete and fully visible, centred, with even margin on all sides. Do not crop the character at any edge. It fills about 75 percent of the square.
Expression: calm, friendly, a small closed smile. Sitting upright.
Style: simplification, cuteness and lovable baby-like appeal are the strongest qualities. Large soft forms, compact proportions, thick rounded contours, an ultra-clean graphic treatment. Prefer one clear shape over several explanatory details. Add an extremely subtle, almost imperceptible sense of depth.
Constraints: no text or watermark. No borders, frames, cards or presentation masks. One character only, no extra subjects or scenery. No fragile lines, sharp tips, unnecessary outlines, tiny details or decorative marks. No photorealistic material, dramatic bevel, glossy hotspot, deep occlusion, extrusion, strong three-dimensional rendering, or external cast shadow. Background solid and uniform, with no texture, vignette or lighting variation.
```
