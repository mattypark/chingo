ChinGo Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 September 2026

The short version. ChinGo runs on your phone. Your photos, the people you have caught, and the places you have been are stored on your device and are not uploaded to us. We have no servers holding your data, no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics.

Who we are

ChinGo is made by HMU, INC. Questions about this policy or your data: matthew.parkk0@gmail.com.

What the app uses, and why

  • Location, while you are using the app. Used to draw the map around you, to resurface places you have been with a friend, and to tell whether you are physically next to someone you are catching. ChinGo never asks for background location.
  • Camera. Used only when you take a photo as part of catching someone.
  • Your date of birth, once. Checked at signup to confirm you are old enough. We do not keep the date — only whether you passed.

Where it goes

Nowhere. Catches, photos, friends and memories are written to storage on your device. The app makes one kind of network request: downloading map tiles from OpenFreeMap so the map can draw. That request tells their server which map squares to send, and nothing about who you are.

We do not sell data, share it with advertisers or data brokers, or use it for tracking. The app does not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt because it does not track you.

What other people can see

This is the part that matters most, so it is worth being exact:

  • Nobody ever sees your precise location. Not friends, not us.
  • You are only findable at all while you have switched yourself visible. That is off by default and is a per-session choice, not a setting you forget about.
  • Even then, you appear as a coarse area, not a point, and only when enough other people are in that same area that you cannot be picked out of it.
  • Nobody is ever added to someone's album without agreeing. Every catch needs both people to accept.
  • Your memories are yours. The friend in a photo does not get access to your copy.

Facial recognition

ChinGo does not use facial recognition and does not create face scans, faceprints, or any other biometric identifier. Photos are images, and nothing derives biometric data from them. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated first and consent will be asked for separately.

Age

ChinGo is for people aged 16 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Deleting your data

Because everything lives on your device, deleting the app deletes your data with it. When accounts arrive, the app will include a way to delete your account and everything attached to it from inside the app itself.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing — we do not sell or share it. Precise location counts as sensitive personal information under California law; ChinGo uses it only to provide the features described above. To make a request, email the address at the top of this page.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what happens to your data, the app will say so rather than quietly updating this page.
